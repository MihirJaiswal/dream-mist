## The screen

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Top bar | The city name and menu, **Build** and **Play**, the command search, **Publish**, **Share**, your party and trainer card, and your account. |
| Tool rail | On the far left: Brush, Eraser, Rectangle, Fill, Picker and Select, with Undo and Redo at the bottom. |
| Side panel | **Maps** (outside and interiors), **Layers**, and the options of the selected layer. |
| Map controls | At the bottom right: zoom, center, grid, minimap and side panel toggles. |
| Minimap | Top right. Click or drag in it to jump around. |
| Status bar | At the bottom: the map you're on, the tile under the cursor, where the town is saved, and **Problems**. |

Hover any icon to see what it does and its shortcut. Drag the edge of the side panel to resize it, and double-click the edge to hide it.

## Command search

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows), or the magnifying glass in the top bar, and start typing. It finds any action (Play, Share, Version history, Export…), tool, layer, stamp, terrain or favorite, map, person on the current map, and current problem. Use the arrow keys and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to run one.

## Problems

The status bar counts what looks broken in your town. Click it for the list, then click a problem to go straight to it: the editor opens the right map and layer, selects the person, door or story step, and centers on it. It checks:

- **Doors** that lead nowhere yet, or to a deleted map.
- **Interiors** nobody can get into, or that have no door back out.
- **People** with nothing to say, blank signs, and trainers with no Pokémon.
- **Visitors** arriving on a blocked tile.
- **Encounter zones** painted on the map but with no Pokémon.
- **Story steps** that can never happen, because nobody completes them or whoever does waits for a step that never happens, plus gates and people left from deleted steps.

## Tools

| Tool | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brush | <kbd>B</kbd> | Paints the current brush or stamp. Drag to paint a line. |
| Eraser | <kbd>E</kbd> | Removes tiles from the current layer. |
| Rectangle | <kbd>R</kbd> | Drag to fill a rectangle. Multi-tile stamps repeat as a pattern. |
| Fill | <kbd>F</kbd> | Fills a connected area of the same tile. |
| Picker | <kbd>I</kbd> | Click a tile on the map to use it as the brush. |
| Select | <kbd>S</kbd> | Drag to select an area to copy, cut, paste or clear. |

Tools work on the selected layer. On gameplay layers like Collision and Encounters they paint that layer's value instead of tiles.

## Layers

Select a layer in the side panel or with <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd>. Hover a layer to show two buttons:

- **Eye**: hide the layer while you edit. Visitors always see every layer.
- **Lock**: prevent painting on it by mistake.

See [How a game works](/docs/how-it-works#layers) for what each layer holds.

## Moving around

| Do this | With |
| --- | --- |
| Pan | Two-finger swipe, right-drag, or <kbd>Space</kbd>+drag. On a touch screen, drag with two fingers |
| Zoom | Pinch, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll, <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>−</kbd> |
| Fit the map | <kbd>0</kbd> or the center button |
| Jump | Click or drag in the minimap |
| Toggle the grid | <kbd>G</kbd> |

On a tablet, one finger paints and two fingers pan and zoom. Buttons get bigger, the delete and show/hide buttons that normally appear on hover stay visible, and on small screens the side panel starts closed so the map has room.

Zoom goes from 50% to 800% and stays anchored under your cursor. The zoom label in the map controls resets it to 300%.

## Selecting, copying and pasting

Press <kbd>S</kbd> and drag on the map. A selection takes **every layer** of the area: tiles, collisions, encounter zones and story gates. That makes it easy to duplicate a whole house, a fenced garden or a patch of grass.

- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> copies, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>X</kbd> cuts, <kbd>Delete</kbd> clears.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>V</kbd> enters paste mode: click to place as many copies as you like, <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop.
- **Use as brush** turns the selection into a stamp you can paint with.

People and doors are not copied. Place them again after pasting.

## Undo and redo

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> redoes. Every change is undoable: painting, people, story steps and doors, and also renaming the town or an interior, resizing a map, removing an interior, and adding or removing tilesets, sprites and music. Opening another map starts a fresh history.

For towns saved online, **Version history** goes back further. See [Accounts and saving](/docs/saving).

## The city menu

The arrow next to the city name opens:

- **City settings**: map size, music and tilesets.
- **Your cities**: every city on this device and in your account. Open, delete, or import a `.json` file.
- **Version history**: earlier copies of a town saved online, to roll back to.
- **New city**: start from the starter town, an empty field, or one of 60 real Hoenn maps.
- **Export image**: a PNG of the current map at full size.
- **Export .json**: the whole city as a file you can back up or import later.
- **Turn sound off** or on, **Explore cities**, **Docs** and **DreamMist home**.

## Starting from Hoenn

**New city** lists 60 towns, cities and routes from Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald. They come with their tiles, collisions, wild Pokémon zones from the real games, and people in their original spots. Collisions are worked out from the tiles, so check them before sharing.
