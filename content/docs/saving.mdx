## Saving

Your city saves on its own while you build. Nothing to press.

|                          | Without an account                | With an account                                 |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Cities                   | In this browser                   | Online, on every device                         |
| Trainer progress         | In this browser                   | Online, on every device                         |
| Share links              | The whole city packed in the link | Short links that always show the latest version |
| Custom tilesets          | Up to 512 KB, stored in the city  | Up to 4 MB, uploaded                            |
| Custom music             | Not available                     | Up to 12 MB per song                            |
| Publish, likes and forks | Not available                     | Available                                       |
| Live rooms               | Join a friend's room as a guest   | Start rooms and invite builders                 |

<Callout type="warn">
  Browser saves disappear if you clear your browser data or use a private window. Log in, or export your city as `.json`, to keep a backup.
</Callout>

## Where is my town saved?

The badge next to the city name in the top bar always tells you:

| Badge               | What it means                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Saved               | In your account. Click it to see your cities.                                         |
| Live                | In a live room, saved for everyone as you build.                                      |
| Only on this device | You're logged out, so it's only in this browser. Click it to log in.                  |
| Save to account     | You're logged in, but this town isn't in your account yet. Click it to save it there. |

While you build logged out, DreamMist reminds you once per visit, at the bottom of the map, that your town only lives in this browser.

## Starting a new town

**New city** in the city menu saves the town you're on first, then opens the new one. Your old town stays in **Your cities**. When you're logged in, the new town goes straight into your account. When you're logged out, the dialog warns you that both towns are only in this browser, with a button to log in first.

## Logging in

Press **Log in** in the top bar and continue with Google. Your username comes from your Google name and is shown on cities you publish.

The sign-in window explains why you're being asked, for example when you publish, invite builders or ask for a short link, then lets you continue with Google.

After you log in:

- **Your trainer**: the first time, the trainer from this browser is saved to your account. If your account already has a trainer, that one is used instead.
- **Your towns**: if this browser has towns that aren't in your account, **Bring your towns along** lists them, all ticked. Save them all, pick some, or choose **Not now**. You can bring them later from your account panel, which reminds you how many are still only in this browser.

**Log out** from your account panel asks first. Your towns stay in your account, and the copies in this browser stay too.

An account can keep up to 50 cities online.

## Version history

For towns saved online, the city menu has **Version history**. A copy of your town is kept as you build, at most every 30 minutes, and the last 20 copies are kept. Pick one and press **Restore** to bring your town back to how it was then. What's there before you restore is kept as a copy too, so a restore can be undone the same way. You can't restore while building together in a live room.

Towns saved only in this browser have no version history: use undo, or export a `.json` backup.

## Your cities

Open **Your cities** from the city menu to see:

- **On this device**: cities saved in this browser. Open or delete them, or save one to your account.
- **Online**: cities in your account. Deleting one also stops its share links from working.

## Export and import

- **Export .json** (city menu or Share) downloads the whole city: every map, person, story step, door, tileset and music link.
- **Import file** in **Your cities** opens a `.json` file as a new city.
- **Export image** downloads a PNG of the current map at full size. Great for previews and posting your map.

See [City file format](/docs/city-file) for what's inside the `.json` file.
