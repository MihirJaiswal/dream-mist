title: Doors and interiors
description: Houses, Pokémon Centers, gyms and rooms you can walk into.
---

A city has an **Outside** map and up to 40 **interiors**. They're listed under **Maps** at the top of the side panel. Click one to edit it.

## Add an interior

There are two ways:

- **From a door**: select the **Doors** layer (<kbd>7</kbd>), click the door of a building, and in **Leads to** choose **+ New house**, **+ New Pokémon Center** or **+ New gym**. The door is linked right away.
- **From the Maps list**: press **+ Interior** and pick a template, then add a door to it.

| Template | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| House | A tiled floor, and a Mom who walks around. |
| Pokémon Center | A stone floor and a Nurse who heals. |
| Gym | A brick floor and a Gym Leader with a Geodude and a Nosepass. |
| Empty room | A stone floor and nobody inside. |

Every template is 14 × 10 tiles with walls around it and an exit door at the bottom that leads back outside. Change the size in **City settings** while the interior is open.

Rename an interior in the Maps list. The trash button deletes it, along with every door leading to it.

## Doors

A door is a tile that moves the player to another map when they step on it.

- Click a tile on the **Doors** layer to add a door, then pick where it **leads to**.
- Click an existing door to change it. **Open** jumps to its destination, **Remove door** deletes it.
- Doors inside an interior can lead **Outside** or to another interior, so you can chain rooms together, like a two-floor house.

### Where the player arrives

The player arrives one tile away from the door that leads back where they came from. So when a house's exit door leads outside, and the house's outside door leads into the house, walking in and out just works.

If the destination has no door back, the player arrives at its spawn point.

<Callout title="One door per interior">
  If several doors outside lead into the same interior, the player always comes out of the first one. Give each building its own interior.
</Callout>

## What interiors share

Interiors have their own tiles, collisions, people, doors, spawn point and story gates. They share the city's **story**, **wild Pokémon zones**, **tilesets** and **music**.

## Ideas

- A **cave**: an empty room with a dark floor, rocks on the Objects layer and a cave encounter zone.
- A **two-floor house**: two interiors with a staircase door between them.
- A **locked gym**: story gates on the tile in front of the gym door outside.
- A **secret base**: a door hidden behind trees that leads to a room with a reward giver.
