title: Trainers and gyms
description: Teams, line of sight, prize money and badges.

Add a person in the **People** layer and set **Role** to **Trainer (battles)**. Trainer settings appear under it.

## Team

A trainer has one to six Pokémon. For each one, type its name and set its level (1 to 100). They are sent out in order, so put the strongest last.

Trainer Pokémon know the last four moves they would have learned by that level, from their real level-up moves. They are never shiny.

## Line of sight

Trainers see **4 tiles straight ahead** in the direction they face. When the player steps into that line, the trainer shows a **!**, walks up, says their dialogue and starts the battle. Walls and anything on the Collision layer block their view.

- Face trainers across a path so the player can't avoid them, or to the side so they can.
- A trainer who **walks around** changes direction, so their line of sight moves too.
- Players can also start the battle by talking to the trainer.

## Settings

| Setting       | What it does                                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Dialogue      | Said before the battle.                                                  |
| When defeated | Said when they lose, and every time the player talks to them afterwards. |
| Gym leader    | Awards a badge when they lose.                                           |
| Badge name    | The badge's name. Without one, it's named after the map.                 |

<Callout type="warn" title="Name your badges">
  When the Gym Leader stands inside an interior called *Gym*, the default badge name is *Gym Badge*. Set **Badge name** to something like *Mist Badge*.
</Callout>

## Winning and losing

| Result            | What happens                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Beat a trainer    | The player gets 2 Poké Balls. The trainer won't battle them again in this city. |
| Beat a Gym Leader | The player gets the badge and 5 Poké Balls. Badges appear on the trainer card.  |
| Lose              | The party is healed and the player goes back to the spawn point of the map.     |

Players can't run from trainer battles, and can't throw Poké Balls at a trainer's Pokémon.

If the player has no Pokémon able to fight, trainers tell them to go heal first instead of battling.

## Trainers in the story

A trainer's **Story** settings work like everyone else's, with one difference: **Beating them completes** a step, instead of talking.

- **Needs first**: the trainer doesn't spot the player and won't battle until the step is done. Use it for Gym Leaders who wait for the player to be ready.
- **Shows up**: for rivals who arrive later.
- **Reward**: given right after the battle, on top of Poké Balls and the badge.

## A gym

The quickest way to make a gym is a **door** that leads to **+ New gym**. It creates a room with a brick floor and a Gym Leader with a Geodude and a Nosepass. Then:

1. Change the leader's team, levels and badge name.
2. Add one or two gym trainers on the way to the leader, facing the path.
3. Optionally, set the leader's **Beating them completes** to a step that opens the next area.

See [Design a good game](/docs/game-design#balance-the-levels) for level suggestions.
