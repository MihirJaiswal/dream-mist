---
title: Story recipes
description: Copy-ready patterns for quests, blocked paths, rivals, rewards and endings.
---

Each recipe uses only the **Story** layer and a person's **Story** settings. To learn what each setting does, see [Story and events](/docs/story).

## The Professor gives you a Pokémon

The player gets a second partner at the start of the game.

1. Add a step _Meet the Professor_.
2. Add the Professor. In **Story**, set **Talking completes** to _Meet the Professor_.
3. In **Reward**, pick the Pokémon and its level, for example Ralts at level 5.
4. In **Says afterwards**: _“Take good care of it!”_

The reward is given once per player. If the party already has six Pokémon, it goes to the PC box.

## Cut the small tree

A tree blocks a shortcut until the player has met someone.

1. Add a step _Learn Cut_. Choose **Blocked by → Object → Small tree**.
2. Paint gate tiles where the tree should stand.
3. Add a person who **completes** _Learn Cut_ with the dialogue _“Here, I'll teach your Pokémon how to cut small trees.”_
4. Set the step's message to _“A small tree. Maybe it could be cut down…”_

## The sleeping Snorlax

A Snorlax blocks the road. The player can battle it, catch it, and then the road is open.

1. Select the **People** layer, choose the **Pokémon** tab, and click the middle of the road.
2. Click its image and pick Snorlax. Set its level to 30.
3. Under **When the player talks to it**, choose **Wild battle**.
4. Write the message _“Snorlax is fast asleep… It woke up!”_

Once beaten or caught, Snorlax is gone and the road is free. To open more of the map at the same time, add a step and set Snorlax's **Beating or catching it completes** to it.

## A legendary at the end

1. Build a small room behind a locked door or a story gate.
2. Place a **Pokémon** with a **Wild battle**, like Latias at level 40.
3. Set its **Shows up** to the last step of your story, so it only appears at the end.

## The guard who wants a badge

A person stands in the road and only moves once the gym is beaten.

1. Add a step _Win the badge_. Choose **Blocked by → Person**.
2. Press **Place a person in the way** and click the road.
3. Press **Edit** on the new person. Give them the dialogue _“Only trainers with a badge may pass.”_
4. Set the Gym Leader's **Beating them completes** to _Win the badge_.

The guard fades away right after the badge is earned.

## The rival who shows up later

1. Add a rival with **Role → Trainer**.
2. Set **Shows up** to the step before the rival's scene, for example _Meet the Professor_.
3. Optionally set **Beating them completes** to a step like _Beat your rival_ and use it to open the next area.

Trainers who show up later still spot the player from 4 tiles away, so place them where the player will walk past.

## A trainer who isn't ready yet

A Gym Leader who won't battle until the player has done something else.

1. Set the leader's **Needs first** to a step like _Beat your rival_.
2. Write **Says until then**: _“Come back when you've proven yourself!”_

Until that step is done, the leader doesn't spot the player and says the waiting line when spoken to.

## A chain of tasks

Make the player do things in order: get a parcel, deliver it, receive a reward.

1. Add steps _Get the parcel_ and _Deliver the parcel_.
2. The shopkeeper **completes** _Get the parcel_.
3. The Professor **Needs first** _Get the parcel_ and **completes** _Deliver the parcel_, with a reward of 10 Poké Balls.

Steps are only finished by the person who completes them. **Needs first** is what keeps them in order.

## Someone who leaves town

A person who's there at the start and gone later, like a Mom who goes to the market.

- Set **Leaves** to the step after which they're gone.

Combine it with **Shows up** on a second copy of the person somewhere else to make them “move” across the map.

## A message that changes

A villager says one thing before a step and another after it, without being part of the quest themselves.

1. Set **Needs first** to the step.
2. Write the line before it in **Says until then**, and the line after it in **Dialogue**.

When the person completes their own step, use **Says afterwards** instead: **Dialogue** is said the first time, **Says afterwards** every time after.

## A road that opens at the end

1. Add a final step like _Become champion_, blocked by a **Boulder** object.
2. Paint the gates across the exit.
3. The last trainer **completes** it.
4. Add a person who **Shows up** after it to congratulate the player and point to the open road.

## A secret area

1. Paint a dead end with trees, and a gap hidden under an **Above** layer tree top.
2. Leave the gap walkable on the **Collision** layer.
3. Put a reward giver or a rare encounter zone behind it.

Players who explore find it, everyone else walks past.

## A door that opens later

Paint story gates on the tile in front of a door. The door can't be reached until the step is done, so it works for locked gyms, caves and secret rooms.
