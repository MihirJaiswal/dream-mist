DreamMist lets you make a small Pokémon game without writing code. You paint a town with the real Generation 3 Hoenn tileset, fill it with people, trainers and wild Pokémon, write a story that opens the map step by step, and add houses, Pokémon Centers and gyms you can walk into. Then you press **Play** and it works like the original games: walking, talking, battles, catching, badges.

When it's ready, publish it. Friends can play it from a link, like it on [Explore](/explore), or fork it to build their own version on top.

## What you can make

- **A starter town**: a few houses, a Professor who hands out a Pokémon, a route full of tall grass.
- **A gym town**: trainers to beat on the way, a Gym Leader with a team, a badge, and a road that only opens once you have it.
- **A small adventure**: several story steps, people who show up later, a rival, blocked paths, rewards and interiors.
- **A remix**: open one of the 60 real Hoenn towns and routes and change it, or fork a friend's city.

## Try it in five minutes

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the editor

Press **Start building**. A starter town opens with trees, paths, a Pokémon Center, a house and some tall grass. It saves on its own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Place something

In the side panel pick a stamp like **House** or **Tree** and click on the map. Drag with two fingers or right-drag to move around, pinch or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll to zoom.

</Step>
<Step>

### Play it

Press **Play** in the top bar. Choose a name, a look and your partner (Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip). Walk with <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> and step into the tall grass to meet wild Pokémon.

</Step>
<Step>

### Go back to building

Press **Build** to keep editing. Switching between Build and Play is instant, so test as often as you like.

</Step>
</Steps>
