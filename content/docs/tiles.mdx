Select the **Ground**, **Objects** or **Above** layer to see the brushes in the side panel. There are four ways to pick what you paint.

## Stamps

Ready-made pieces that go on the right layer and set their own collisions.

| Stamp                            | Layer                 | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Grass                            | Ground                | Plain short grass.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Tall grass (wild Pokémon)        | Objects               | Stands on the grass and stays walkable. Also paints the **Tall grass** encounter zone; erasing it or painting over it removes the zone.                                                           |
| Sand path, Gravel path, Pavement | Ground                | Walkable paths.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Water                            | Ground                | Blocks movement.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Flowers                          | Objects               | Walkable decoration.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Tree, Pine tree                  | Objects, top on Above | 2 × 2. The trunk blocks movement; the top goes on the Above layer, so trees can overlap like a forest and the player can walk just behind them. Erasing the trunk on Objects removes the top too. |
| Sign                             | Objects               | The sign post. Add a sign person on it to make it readable.                                                                                                                                       |
| House, Small house               | Objects               | Buildings with a door tile. Add a door to make them enterable.                                                                                                                                    |
| Lab, Pokémon Center, Poké Mart   | Objects               | Classic buildings from Littleroot and Oldale.                                                                                                                                                     |

## Terrains

Terrains draw their own borders. Paint a patch and the corners and edges are picked for you as you go, even when you paint next to an existing patch.

- **Light grass**, **Sand** and **Dirt** for outdoor areas.
- **Stone floor**, **Tiled floor** and **Brick floor** for interiors.

## The tileset palette

Under the stamps is the full Hoenn tileset, 6,328 tiles from the Generation 3 games.

- **Drag across it** to pick a group of tiles and paint them as one stamp. The editor switches to the layer those tiles usually sit on in the real Hoenn maps: grass and paths go on **Ground**, trees and houses on **Objects**. You can still pick another layer afterwards. Tiles with see-through parts never go on **Ground**, where nothing is drawn under them and the clear parts would show black. Picking the exact tiles of a stamp, like a whole tree, works like the stamp.
- **All / Ground / Objects / Above** above the palette dims the tiles that don't belong to that layer, so the ones you need stand out. Groups keep their place, so a house is still in one piece.
- **Pinch** or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll on the palette to zoom it. The zoom buttons and reset button are in its header.
- **Drag the handle** under the palette to make it taller.
- **Recent** keeps the last brushes you used.

If you imported your own tilesets, they show up as tabs next to **Hoenn**. See [Custom tilesets and music](/docs/custom-assets).

## Emerald tilesets

Press **+ Emerald** next to the tileset tabs to add any of the 67 tilesets of Pokémon Emerald: every town (Petalburg, Rustboro, Fortree…), every gym, caves, the Pokémon Center, the Battle Frontier and more. Search by name, pick one, and it opens as a new tab, its tiles 16 pixels like the rest.

- **Shared: General** holds what most outdoor maps use: grass, trees, water, ledges, paths.
- A town's own tileset adds its buildings and details.
- Tiles keep the see-through parts they have in the game, so put pieces with holes on **Objects** or **Above**, over ground.

They come from the [pret/pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald) decompilation and are served with DreamMist, so they don't count against your uploads.

## Overlapping objects

Each layer holds one tile per square. When you paint a piece with see-through parts, like the edge of a rock, a roof corner or another tree, over a square where a tree, rock or building already stands, the new piece goes on the **Above** layer over it instead of wiping it out. What you paint last ends up on top, and anything that blocks still blocks. Erasing on **Objects** removes the stacked piece too.

Small walkable decoration like flowers and tall grass is simply replaced.

To change which one is in front, right-click where they overlap and choose **Bring to front** or **Send to back**. Each shows a small picture of the piece it moves. It changes the whole overlap at once, every connected square where the two pieces meet, and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes it.

## Search and favorites

- **Search** above the stamps finds stamps, terrains and favorites by name. Tiles in the palette have no names, so they don't show up in search: save them as favorites first.
- **Favorites**: pick anything, then press the star next to the current brush and give it a name, like _Blue roof house_. Favorites appear above the stamps and in search. Hover one and click × to remove it. They're kept in this browser and shared by all your cities. Only Hoenn tiles can be favorites, not tiles from imported tilesets.

## Random mode

The shuffle button next to the current brush turns on **random mode**: each cell gets a random tile of the brush instead of the pattern. Pick a few grass or flower tiles from the palette, turn it on, and paint natural-looking fields.

## Picker

Press <kbd>I</kbd> and click any tile on the map to use it as the brush. It picks from the selected layer, or from the top-most layer with a tile.

## Above layer tips

Tiles on the **Above** layer are drawn over the player, and they never block movement. Use them for:

- Roofs, so the player can walk behind a house.
- Tree tops at the bottom edge of a forest.
- Bridges or arches the player walks under.
