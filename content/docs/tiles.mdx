---
title: Tiles, stamps and terrains
description: Everything you can paint with, and how to pick it.
---

Select the **Ground**, **Objects** or **Above** layer to see the brushes in the side panel. There are four ways to pick what you paint.

## Stamps

Ready-made pieces that go on the right layer and set their own collisions.

| Stamp | Layer | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grass | Ground | Plain short grass. |
| Tall grass (wild Pokémon) | Ground | Also paints the **Tall grass** encounter zone. Erasing it removes the zone. |
| Sand path, Gravel path, Pavement | Ground | Walkable paths. |
| Water | Ground | Blocks movement. |
| Flowers | Objects | Walkable decoration. |
| Tree, Pine tree | Objects | 2 × 2, blocks movement. |
| Sign | Objects | The sign post. Add a sign person on it to make it readable. |
| House, Small house | Objects | Buildings with a door tile. Add a door to make them enterable. |
| Lab, Pokémon Center, Poké Mart | Objects | Classic buildings from Littleroot and Oldale. |

## Terrains

Terrains draw their own borders. Paint a patch and the corners and edges are picked for you as you go, even when you paint next to an existing patch.

- **Light grass**, **Sand** and **Dirt** for outdoor areas.
- **Stone floor**, **Tiled floor** and **Brick floor** for interiors.

## The tileset palette

Under the stamps is the full Hoenn tileset, 6,328 tiles from the Generation 3 games.

- **Drag across it** to pick a group of tiles and paint them as one stamp.
- **Pinch** or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll on the palette to zoom it. The zoom buttons and reset button are in its header.
- **Drag the handle** under the palette to make it taller.
- **Recent** keeps the last brushes you used.

If you imported your own tilesets, they show up as tabs next to **Hoenn**. See [Custom tilesets and music](/docs/custom-assets).

## Random mode

The shuffle button next to the current brush turns on **random mode**: each cell gets a random tile of the brush instead of the pattern. Pick a few grass or flower tiles from the palette, turn it on, and paint natural-looking fields.

## Picker

Press <kbd>I</kbd> and click any tile on the map to use it as the brush. It picks from the selected layer, or from the top-most layer with a tile.

## Above layer tips

Tiles on the **Above** layer are drawn over the player, and they never block movement. Use them for:

- Roofs, so the player can walk behind a house.
- Tree tops at the bottom edge of a forest.
- Bridges or arches the player walks under.
