<Callout title="New here? Start with the tour">
  The first time you open the editor, a short tour walks you through naming your town, painting a path, adding someone, giving them a line and talking to them
  in Play. It takes about two minutes. Open it again anytime from the city menu: **Getting started tour**.
</Callout>

This guide builds a complete little game called **Mistwood Town**. It takes about half an hour and touches almost every feature. Here is the plan:

1. The player wakes up at home. The roads out of town are closed.
2. The **Professor** in the Lab lets them choose a first Pokémon, and the roads open.
3. On the route south, wild Pokémon live in the tall grass and a Youngster wants to battle.
4. The **rival** shows up and challenges them. A Hiker blocks the gym until the rival is beaten.
5. The **Gym Leader** gives the _Mist Badge_, and a boulder blocking the way north is gone.

<Callout title="Test as you go">
  Press **Play** after each part. If a step is already done and you want to try it again, open the **Story** layer and press **Restart my progress**.
</Callout>

## Part 1: the town

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the city

Open the city menu (the arrow next to the city name) and choose **New city**. Name it _Mistwood Town_ and pick **Starter town**. You get trees around the edge, two paths that lead out on every side (where routes can join), a Pokémon Center with a Nurse inside, the player's house with their room upstairs, the Professor's Lab, a sign and a patch of tall grass.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make room for a route

Open **City settings** from the city menu. Set the size to **40 × 60** and press **Resize**. The town stays at the top and new grass appears below it: that's where the route goes. The gates that closed the bottom path now sit in the middle of the map: open the **Story** layer and erase them there, then paint new ones across the path at the bottom of town.

</Step>
<Step>

### Where the player starts

Under **City settings → New game**, new players start in the **Player's room**, upstairs in their house. Write the Professor's welcome in **Professor's welcome** if you like: a new player hears it before the game starts.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: the story

The starter town's story already has a first step, **Get your first Pokémon**: gates close every way out of town until the Professor hands the player a Pokémon.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add two steps

Select the **Story** layer (<kbd>6</kbd>) and press **Add step** twice. Rename them:

2. _Beat your rival_
3. _Win the Mist Badge_

</Step>
<Step>

### Block the way north with a boulder

Click step 3, choose **Object** and **Boulder**, and paint gates across the north exit of the town. Change the message to something like _“A huge boulder. The Gym Leader said they would move it for champions.”_

</Step>
</Steps>

## Part 3: the Professor

<Steps>
<Step>

### Meet him

Under **Maps** in the side panel, click **Pokémon Lab**, select the **People** layer (<kbd>8</kbd>) and click the Professor. His **Script** lets the player choose Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip (**Choose a starter**), then completes _Get your first Pokémon_. It plays once; after that he says his **Dialogue**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make him your own

Change his **Name** to _Professor Birch_ and the Pokémon in **Choose a starter** to the three you want. Add a **Give item** after it, with **5 Poké Balls**, so the player can catch Pokémon right away.

</Step>
<Step>

### Try it

Click **Outside** under **Maps**, then press **Play**. You wake up in your room. The paths out of town are closed. Go to the Lab, choose your partner, and walk back out: the way is open.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Part 4: the route

<Steps>
<Step>

### Paint the route

Below the town, use the **Sand path** or **Light grass** terrain to draw a winding path. Line it with **Tree** stamps. Add a few patches of **Tall grass (wild Pokémon)**: this stamp paints the grass and its encounter zone at the same time.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick the wild Pokémon

Select the **Encounters** layer (<kbd>5</kbd>). The **Tall grass** zone already has Zigzagoon, Wurmple, Poochyena and Ralts at levels 2 to 5. Change them if you like: type a name in the Pokémon box and set the level range. Keep the **Encounter rate** around 10 to 15%.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a route trainer

Select **People** and click an empty tile next to the path. Name them _Youngster Joey_, pick the youngster look, set **Role** to **Trainer** and face them across the path. Give them one Pokémon, like Zigzagoon at level 5. Trainers see 4 tiles in front of them and walk up to the player when they spot them.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Part 5: the rival and the gym

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add the rival

Back in town, add a person called _Wally_. Set **Role** to **Trainer** with a team of two Pokémon around level 6 and 7. In **Story**:

- **Shows up**: _1. Get your first Pokémon_, so they arrive after the Lab.
- **Beating them completes**: _2. Beat your rival_.
- **Says afterwards**: _“I'll get stronger, just you wait!”_

</Step>
<Step>

### Build the gym

Place a **House** stamp where the gym should be. Select **Doors**, click its door and choose **+ New gym**. The gym comes with a Gym Leader who has a Geodude and a Nosepass.

</Step>
<Step>

### Tune the Gym Leader

Open the gym and click the leader. Lower the team to around levels 9 and 11 so a player who caught a few Pokémon on the route can win. Check **Gym leader** is ticked and set **Badge name** to _Mist Badge_. In **Story**, set **Beating them completes** to _3. Win the Mist Badge_.

</Step>
<Step>

### Guard the gym door

Go back outside, select the **Story** layer and click step 2. Choose **Blocked by → Person** and press **Place a person in the way**. Click the tile in front of the gym door. A Hiker appears there. Press **Edit** to change their dialogue to _“The Gym Leader only sees trainers who beat their rival.”_ They walk away once step 2 is done.

</Step>
<Step>

### Visit the Pokémon Center

The starter town's Pokémon Center already has a door: press **Play**, walk into it and talk to the Nurse across the counter to heal the party. Buildings you add yourself get one the same way: select **Doors**, click the building's door and pick a room under **Leads to**, like **+ New Pokémon Center** or **+ New Poké Mart**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Part 6: finish and share

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add a reward for the end

Outside, add a person near the boulder with **Shows up** set to _3. Win the Mist Badge_ and the dialogue _“You did it! The road north is open.”_

</Step>
<Step>

### Play it from the start

In the Story layer press **Restart my progress**, then **Play** and go through the whole game. Check that every gate holds, every trainer can be beaten, and the player can always get back to a Nurse.

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish

Press **Publish** in the top bar, write a short description and publish. Your game shows up on [Explore](/explore), where anyone can play it or fork it.

</Step>
</Steps>
