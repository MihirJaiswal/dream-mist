Press **Play** in the top bar to play the city you're building, or open a visit link to play someone else's.

## Your trainer

The first time you press Play, you create a trainer:

- **Name**: up to 16 characters.
- **Looks**: one of 16 trainer sprites.
- **Partner**: Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip, at level 5.

You start with **10 Poké Balls** and **5 Potions**. Your trainer comes with you to every city you play.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or the arrow keys |
| Run | Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> (twice as fast) |
| Talk, read, continue | <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Z</kbd> or <kbd>E</kbd> |
| Pick a battle option | Click it, or press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd> |

On phones and tablets a direction pad and an **A** button appear on screen.

Walk onto a door to go inside. Tall grass hides your trainer's feet, and every step in it can start a wild battle.

## Party and PC box

The **Party** button in the top bar shows your Pokémon with their level, types, HP and moves.

- **Lead** sends a Pokémon first in battle.
- **To box** moves it to the PC box, **To party** brings it back. You can carry six.
- Your bag shows how many Poké Balls and Potions you have.

Caught Pokémon go to the party, or straight to the box when the party is full.

## Trainer card

The **Trainer card** button shows your Pokédex (seen and caught), how many trainers you've beaten, your badges with the city and leader who gave them, and every Pokémon you've caught.

**Start over** erases your trainer, party, badges and story progress, so you can pick a new partner.

## Healing

- Talk to a **Nurse** to heal the whole party. Nurses also top up Poké Balls and Potions to at least 5 each.
- Use a **Potion** in battle to restore 20 HP.
- If all your Pokémon faint, you go back to the start of the map, healed.

## Progress

| What | Where it's kept |
| --- | --- |
| Trainer, party, box, bag, Pokédex, badges | One profile for every city |
| Story steps and beaten trainers | Per city |

Without an account, progress is saved in this browser. Log in to keep it online and play from any device. See [Accounts and saving](/docs/saving).
