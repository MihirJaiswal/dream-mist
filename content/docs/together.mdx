## Invite builders

Press **Share**, then **Invite builders**. You get an invite link. Everyone who opens it builds the same city with you, live.

Friends who aren't logged in can join as a guest with a name, or log in to keep the city in their library.

## In a room

- Everyone's **cursor** shows on the map with their name and color.
- **Tiles, collisions, zones, people, story, doors, interiors, tilesets and music** sync instantly.
- The **builders** list in the top bar shows who's here.
- A **chat** box lets you talk while you build. Messages also show up as notifications.
- Changes save automatically. The builder who has been in the room longest saves for everyone; if they leave, the next one takes over.

Each builder can be on a different map: one in the town, one in the gym.

## Managing the room

- **Reset link** creates a new invite link. The old one stops working for new builders.
- **Leave room** stops building together. Your copy stays as it is.

## Tips

- Split the work: one person paints the map, another writes the story and people.
- Agree on story step names first, since people link to them.
