---
title: FAQ and troubleshooting
description: Answers to common questions and fixes for common problems.
---

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="The player walks through walls, trees or water">

Open the **Collision** layer and paint red tiles where the player shouldn't go. Objects block automatically only when **Objects block movement automatically** is on, and custom tiles always need a check. See [Collision and spawn](/docs/collision).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The player can't walk somewhere they should">

Look at the **Collision** layer for red tiles, and at the **Story** layer for gate tiles of a step that isn't done. A person standing there also blocks the tile.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The player walks around my story gate">

Gates only block the tiles you paint. Walk along the gate in Play mode and paint the missing tiles, or close the gaps with trees or fences.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I finished a step and can't test it again">

Press **Restart my progress** in the Story layer. It forgets your finished steps and beaten trainers in this city.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A person doesn't appear in Play mode">

Check their **Shows up** and **Leaves** settings. Someone who shows up after a step only appears once that step is done, and someone who leaves is gone after it. Signs have no sprite, so they're never visible.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A trainer doesn't battle me">

- They only see 4 tiles straight ahead, and walls block their view.
- They don't battle again once beaten. Restart your progress to fight them again.
- They don't battle if they **need** a step you haven't done.
- They don't battle if none of your Pokémon can fight. Heal first.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I never meet wild Pokémon">

The tile needs an encounter zone (check the **Encounters** layer) with at least one Pokémon, and your party needs a Pokémon able to fight. With a low encounter rate it can take a while.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A door says it leads nowhere">

Select the **Doors** layer, click the door and choose a destination in **Leads to**.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I come out of the wrong door">

The player comes out next to the first door that leads into the interior they left. Give each building its own interior.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My share link is very long">

Without an account, the whole city is packed inside the link. Log in and save the city online to get a short link.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My city is gone">

Cities saved without an account live in this browser only. Check **Your cities** in the city menu, and log in to keep cities online. Export `.json` backups of cities you care about.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I use my own art and music?">

Yes. Import tilesets and upload music in **City settings**. See [Custom tilesets and music](/docs/custom-assets).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I make a game with more than one town?">

A city has one outside map and up to 40 interiors, so you can build caves, houses, gyms and rooms in one city. For a bigger world, make several cities and link them from signs or descriptions. Your players' trainers, Pokémon and badges travel with them.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do I need to know how to code?">

No. Everything in a game is built in the editor: maps, people, battles, story and doors.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
